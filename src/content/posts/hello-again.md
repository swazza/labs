I used to write about software architecture here and there. I find myself doing a lot of small experiments to understand how systems actually behave: building pieces of a storage engine, poking at CPU caches, measuring branch prediction, looking at what the operating system is doing, and occasionally questioning assumptions I've carried around for years.

Most of these aren't worthy of an article. But they're worth writing down.

That's what this site is for.

Notes, experiments, measurements, code, diagrams, things that didn't work, and things I understand a little better than I did before.

No particular publishing schedule. No attempt at completeness.

Just a lab notebook that happens to be public.
